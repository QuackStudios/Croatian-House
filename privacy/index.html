Privacy at Croatian House

This notice explains how Croatian House handles the personal information you send through this website. If you have a question about your information, email enquiries@croatianhouse.com.au.

Information you provide

A venue enquiry can include your name, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, venue and event preferences, proposed date, guest estimate and any message you choose to add.

How it is used

Croatian House uses this information to assess your enquiry, check suitability and availability, contact you about the request and manage the enquiry through its operational customer systems.

Service providers

The enquiry form sends information through Zapier to the systems used by the venue team, including HoneyBook and email services. These providers process information to deliver the enquiry workflow; they are not website advertising trackers.

Optional updates

Marketing consent is optional. If you select it, Croatian House may send occasional event and venue updates. You can ask to stop receiving them by replying to a message or contacting the address above.

Access and correction

To ask what information Croatian House holds about you, request a correction or raise a privacy concern, contact enquiries@croatianhouse.com.au.
