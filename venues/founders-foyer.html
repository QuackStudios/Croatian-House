Founders’ Foyer

Ground floor · Arrival for the indoor rooms

Founders’ Foyer is where indoor events at Croatian House begin. Up to 40 guests gather at ground level for welcome drinks, registration or the first exchange before doors open to The Gallery or The Parlour. Included at no charge with every wedding booking, it gives the welcome, guest list and transition into the main room a clear place.

Venue details

Plan the arrival

01 Capacity & layouts

Standing 40. Ground floor. The foyer can be arranged for arrival, welcome drinks, registration, a gift and place-card table, or a receiving line before the doors open.

02 Catering & bar

Founders’ Foyer is an arrival space rather than a separate bar venue. When welcome drinks form part of the main event booking, Croatian House staff pour and serve them in the foyer.

03 Using it with other spaces

Founders’ Foyer opens directly into The Gallery and The Parlour. It is included at no charge with every wedding booking.

04 Arrival, access & parking

The foyer is at ground level, with step-free access from the car park. Parking is available on site.

05 FAQ
How many people can Founders’ Foyer hold?
Up to 40 people standing.
Is the foyer included with a wedding booking?
Yes. It is included at no charge with every wedding booking.
Does Founders’ Foyer have its own bar?
No. It is an arrival space; when welcome drinks are included in the main event booking, Croatian House staff pour and serve them there.
Is Founders’ Foyer accessible?
Yes. It has step-free access from the car park at ground level.
