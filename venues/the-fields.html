The Fields

Five acres for sport, culture and open-air events.

The Fields bring together five acres of open ground in multiple full-size playing areas, with stadium lighting for events after dark. Concerts, festivals, markets, exhibitions and sporting carnivals have all used the grounds, while Croatian House's indoor rooms, kitchens, bar and on-site parking can support the wider event plan.

The ground

Open to the event plan

Nothing on the ground fixes an event to one format.

Multiple full-size playing areas sit across five acres, with stadium lighting supporting planned use after dark. The Fields have hosted concerts, festivals, markets, exhibitions and sporting carnivals, as well as outdoor ceremonies that move indoors afterwards.

Marquees, vendors, stages, bars and spectator areas can be arranged around the event. Croatian House runs catering and bar service on the grounds, while indoor spaces can be included for production, presentations, VIP dining and contingency arrangements, subject to the booking.

Plan the grounds

Venue details

01 Capacity & layouts

The Fields cover five acres across multiple full-size playing areas under stadium lighting. The suitable attendance and layout depend on the event format, applicable approvals and the crowd plan for the day. The venue team reviews those requirements before confirming what the grounds can support.

02 Catering & bar

Croatian House runs catering and bar service on the grounds from the kitchens behind the fields. The house operates the bar under its liquor licence. Menus and service are planned around the event; external catering and BYO alcohol are not available.

03 Planning & infrastructure

The plan can include marquees, vendors, stages, temporary service areas, spectator zones and presentation areas. Power, water and amenities are on site, with any additional provision identified during planning.

Requirements relating to council permits, crowd management, traffic plans and event insurance vary by event and must be established before booking details are finalised.

04 Arrival, access & parking

Vehicles can access the grounds for load-in and bump-out. Parking is available on site for guests, suppliers and teams, and access times are agreed before the event.

05 FAQ
How many people can the grounds hold?
It depends on the layout, applicable approvals and the crowd plan. Share the event format with the venue team so the requirements can be assessed.
Can indoor rooms be used at the same time?
Yes, subject to the booking. They can support catering, production, presentations, VIP dining and contingency arrangements.
Are the grounds only for sport?
No. They have hosted concerts, festivals, markets and exhibitions alongside sporting use.
Who provides the catering?
Croatian House does, on the grounds as well as indoors.
Is parking available?
Yes. Parking is available on site.
