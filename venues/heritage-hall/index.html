From first session to last dance

The room changes purpose across the day while Croatian House keeps the kitchen, bar, staff and floor together.

By day, Heritage Hall takes conferences, presentations and expos of up to 1,000 people in theatre layout. The stage is already in place, and catering is served from the kitchen beside the floor.

By night, the hall is planned for seated dining, presentations and dancing. The house team coordinates the room, menu, bar and service under the same booking, with the final layout agreed for the occasion.

Venue details

Plan the room

01 Capacity & layouts

Banquet 800. Standing 1,200. Theatre 1,000. The dancefloor is 180 square metres. Cabaret, classroom, rounds and mixed dining-and-dancing arrangements are set to an agreed floor plan.

02 Catering & bar

Croatian House caters and pours its own. The kitchen serves from beside the floor, and a full-size bar runs the length of the room, both staffed by the house.

Menus are developed with the organiser and may include plated dinners, shared tables, buffets, morning and afternoon tea, or canapés. External catering and BYO alcohol are not available.

03 Production & staging

The built-in stage can accommodate a band, bridal table, keynote or awards presentation. A dedicated room behind it can be used by performers, production, staff or for storage.

Audiovisual, lighting and production requirements can be arranged as part of the booking.

04 Arrival, access & parking

Heritage Hall is on Level One, reached by lift or the dual curved staircase. Men’s and women’s toilets are on the floor, and on-site parking is available.

05 FAQ
Who provides the catering?
Croatian House provides the kitchen, bar and staff, with the menu developed for the event.
Can an external caterer or alcohol be brought in?
No. Catering and beverages are provided in-house under the house licence.
Is there a stage?
Yes. A built-in stage has a dedicated support room behind it.
Can Heritage Hall be used during the day?
Yes. Conferences, expos, presentations and other daytime functions use the same room.
How do guests reach Level One?
Lift access serves Level One alongside the main staircase.
