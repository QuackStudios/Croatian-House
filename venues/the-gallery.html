Inside and out

The room and patio work together

One ground-floor booking carries an event from arrival on the patio to the program inside.

By day, The Gallery takes conferences, training days, expos and long lunches. Breaks can move to the covered patio while the kitchen serves the room from the same floor.

By night, the space can be planned for seated dining, standing celebrations and dancing. Croatian House coordinates the room, patio, menu, bar and staff under the same booking.

Venue details

Plan both spaces

01 Capacity & layouts

Banquet 350. Standing 500. Theatre 450. The dancefloor is 80 square metres. For a standing function, the room and covered patio can be planned together for up to 500 guests.

The covered patio is included with the room rather than booked separately.

02 Catering & bar

A commercial kitchen serves The Gallery directly, with a bar on the floor. Both are operated and staffed by Croatian House.

Menus are developed with the organiser for seated service, shared tables, buffets, tea breaks or canapés. External catering and BYO alcohol are not available.

03 Indoor and outdoor

Glass doors connect the room to a covered patio. It can be used for arrivals, ceremonies, alfresco service, conference breaks or as a second area later in the event.

04 Arrival, access & parking

The Gallery is on the ground floor and is entered through the foyer shared with The Parlour. Men’s, women’s and accessible toilets are available. The route from the car park to the room is step-free, and on-site parking is available.

05 FAQ
Can a ceremony be held at The Gallery?
Yes. The covered patio can host the ceremony while the reception is arranged inside.
Who provides the catering?
Croatian House provides the catering from the commercial kitchen adjoining the room.
Can an external caterer or alcohol be brought in?
No. Catering and beverages are provided in-house under the house licence.
Can The Parlour be used at the same time?
Yes. Both rooms open from the same foyer and can be booked together.
Is The Gallery accessible?
Yes. The route is step-free and accessible toilets are available.
